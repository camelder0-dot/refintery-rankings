Help Us Build the Tippecanoe Valley School Corporation Alumni History Page
Tippecanoe Valley School Corporation is proud of its rich tradition of excellence, community, and school spirit. As we work to create a comprehensive Alumni History Page, we are inviting alumni, current and former staff members, families, and community supporters to help us preserve and share our history.
We are seeking photographs and historical materials dating back to the earliest years of the school corporation, including:
School and classroom photos
Athletic and game day pictures
Club and organization photos
Performing arts and music events
Graduation and class photos
Homecoming, prom, and other special events
Historical documents and memorabilia
To help us accurately document our history, please include names, dates, locations, graduation years, event details, and any stories or background information associated with the photos.
Together, we can create a lasting archive that honors the students, staff, and community members who have shaped Tippecanoe Valley School Corporation through the years.

[email protected]
Thank you for helping us keep the Valley story alive!
Tippecanoe Valley School Corporation communicates with families through ParentSquare!
Tippecanoe Valley School Corporation communicates with families through ParentSquare, our corporation's primary platform for school news, updates, announcements, and emergency notifications delivered by email, text message, and the ParentSquare app.
ParentSquare creates an account automatically for each parent or guardian using the contact information on file. We encourage families to log in to their accounts, download the mobile app, and customize notification settings to choose how and when they receive updates.
Parent and guardian contact information is synced directly from Skyward. To ensure you receive all school and district communications, please keep your email address and phone numbers current in Skyward.

Superintendent's Message
As we begin another school year, I want to welcome our students, families, staff, and community back to Tippecanoe Valley School Corporation. A new year brings new opportunities to learn, grow, and succeed together.
At TVSC, our mission is Student Success through Literacy, Leadership, and Character Development. We remain committed to providing every student with a safe, supportive, and engaging learning environment where they are challenged to reach their full potential.
Success is built through strong partnerships between our schools, families, and community. Together, we will continue to do whatever it takes to equip ALL students to be successful today, tomorrow, and beyond.
Thank you for your continued support of Tippecanoe Valley Schools. I look forward to an outstanding year filled with learning, growth, and student success!!
Sincerely,
Blaine Conley Ed.S.
