August 19, 2026
Welcome Back, Cougars!
Dear Van Wert City Schools Students and Families,
Welcome to the 2026–2027 school year! There is something special about the start of a new year.  The excitement in our buildings, seeing familiar faces again, welcoming new ones, and knowing we have another opportunity to grow together.
At Van Wert City Schools,
Cougar Pride is bigger than one person.
We believe in working as a team, building trusting relationships, doing the right thing even when it is hard, and helping each other become the best versions of ourselves. It is about
WE > ME
and knowing that each of us has an important role in making our schools a great place to learn, work, and belong.
As we begin this year, I encourage our students, staff, and families to enjoy the journey. Be present. Look for opportunities to make a difference. Take care of one another. Keep your head up and don’t get so busy moving on to the next thing that you miss the great moments happening right in front of you.
We believe in our students. We believe in our staff. We believe in our families. We believe something special happens when we all work together as a Cougar Team.
It’s different here for a reason. Trust the process, enjoy the journey, and let’s make it a great year.
Welcome back and Go, Cougars!
Mark Bagley
Superintendent of Van Wert City Schools

The Wired Educator Podcast
The Wired Educator Podcast, Episode 260:
Leading with Heart, Hustle, and Pride with Mark Bagley |
By Kelly Croy
This is the Wired Educator Podcast, Episode 260! I am so excited for you to hear this conversation. I’m sitting down with a good friend, a colleague, and a truly great leader in Ohio:
Mark Bagley, the Superintendent of Van Wert City Schools.
In this episode, we learn exactly what it takes to lead a district with authenticity and how to make a real difference in the lives of both colleagues and children. Mark is a “servant leader” in the truest sense of the word. Whether it’s picking up trash, helping in a classroom when there are no subs, or standing out at the bus hub every single morning, Mark leads from the trenches.
You are going to love this interview. Buckle up!
