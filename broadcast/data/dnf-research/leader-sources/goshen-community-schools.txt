Committed to Every Student
Goshen Community Schools is located in Goshen, Indiana, and serves approximately 6,000 students and 1,100 staff across 10 school sites.
We are a diverse learning community that welcomes all students. Our academic programs are built to ensure that every student will succeed at Goshen Community Schools. We have a high standard of academic excellence and encourage our students to be involved in athletics, performing arts, co-curricular activities, and clubs throughout their educational experience.
Mr. Jim DuBois
Superintendent

Our Values
Put Students First:
We prioritize students’ safety and educational needs.
Whole Child:
We support each child’s career and life well-being in academic/cognitive, behavioral/employability/social/teaming skills, and physical/neurological.
Craftsman Mindset:
We are committed to working hard with love and dedication to achieve our growth goals over time.  (1% Better for a lifetime)
Integrity:
We act with respect, transparency, and honesty in all our interactions.
Teamwork:
We achieve more by working together, leveraging the power of synergy.
Our Commitments
Financial Commitment 1.0:
We are committed to a well-funded, effective, and efficient financial system that supports "best in class" learning opportunities for our students and maintains appropriate balances in all funds.
Student Achievement Commitment 2.0
:  We are committed to having a top-performing educational system that prepares our graduates for college, careers, and life.
Operational Processes Commitment 3.0
:  We are committed to innovative operations that enhance the "best in class" performance of our students.
People Commitment 4.0
:  We are committed to hiring and retaining innovative, engaged, highly effective leaders, educators, and staff who serve our students and families with industry best practices and passion for their work.
