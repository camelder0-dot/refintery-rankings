Superintendent’s Message
Welcome to the Eastbrook Community Schools Corporation!
Mr. Patrick McLaughlin
Superintendent
pmclaughlin@eastbrook.k12.in.us
Our two elementary schools, Eastbrook North and Eastbrook South, our Eastbrook Middle School and Eastbrook High School are committed to meeting the individual needs of all of our students. There are many curricular, co-curricular and extra-curricular programs and initiatives in place to assist us in our mission of preparing students for continuous learning and productive citizenship.
Our success comes from an unbroken commitment and partnership with our parents, patrons and community members who work hard to hold on to the great values of the Eastbrook community while striving for positive innovations for our future.
Thank you for the opportunity to serve you and your family at Eastbrook Community Schools.
