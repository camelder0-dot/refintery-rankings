Smith Academy for Excellence (SAFE)
is a tuition-free charter school designed to strategically develop elite scholars who exhibit responsibility, dignity, character, and an instinct of service.
​
Our school is distinctive — designed specifically to meet the existing and emerging needs of our community. As scholars are provided with quality academic and character education, they emerge from SAFE well prepared for post-secondary education and equipped to be high-quality, well-rounded citizens who are primed to affect positive change in their community.​
