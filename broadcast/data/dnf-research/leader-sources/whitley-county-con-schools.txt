1 day ago, Seth Slater
Whitley County Consolidated Schools recently reviewed the 2027 budget proposal, and we are proud of the stewardship that allows us to lower the levy in 2027 and maintain it through 2023.
In plain terms, WCCS is lowering debt with less cost to taxpayers during a time of inflation, while still getting what we need for students.
WCCS was applauded by Mr. Schumaker, a Stifel representative, for the "years and years of long-term planning." The district's proposed budget and general obligation bond are able to meet the district's needs, while still reducing the impact on taxpayers. A position that would be envied by many districts across Indiana.
Please take the time to listen to the whole summary. As always, we encourage you to join the conversation. Talk with our team and get involved with our Eagle Advocates program. Learn more at wccsonline.com.

21 days ago, WCCS
As a reminder, WCCS can begin using Synchronous Learning (Virtual Learning) days beginning October 1.
This year, synchronous learning begins at 9:30 a.m. for all students. During synchronous learning, everyone logs in at the same time, listens to the teacher, asks questions and works on activities and assignments together. In addition to providing our students with skills and experiences in a blended learning environment, synchronous learning days also enable us to maintain continuity in student learning and reduce the need for make-up days.
We have listed several frequently asked questions and resources on our website at
https://www.wccsonline.com/page/synchronous
In the event of synchronous learning, please have students online and ready for their school day beginning at 9:30 a.m.
21 days ago, WCCS
Happy National School PTO Day, WCCS!
Today we celebrate the incredible parent groups whose energy, creativity and heart strengthen our schools every single day.
At Mary Raber Montessori, the PTSO works to connect families, support students and staff, and create a positive learning environment for all learners. From hosting family events to showing year‑round appreciation for teachers, they make it easy for families to be involved, as much or as little as they choose.
At Coesse Elementary, the PTO may be small in number, but they are mighty in impact. They support students, staff, and families through family fun nights, after‑school programs, field day, classroom contributions, and ongoing staff support.
Today we honor every volunteer, every parent, every caregiver, and every community member who gives their time to help our schools thrive. Your partnership makes a difference!
Thank you, WCCS PTOs, for all you do!
Every school has a PTO or volunteer opportunities to get involved. We encourage you to complete their volunteer application to begin. More info at wccsonline.com.
