Dr. Brad Yates became Superintendent of Bluffton-Harrison Metropolitan School District in July of 2019 after serving five years as Assistant Superintendent of Bluffton-Harrison M.S.D.  Prior to serving as Assistant Superintendent, Dr. Yates served as Business Manager and Transportation Director for the district from 2011 to 2014.  He began his teaching career as a vocational business education teacher at Bluffton High School in 2006.  Throughout his teaching career, Dr. Yates coached boys' basketball at various levels from 2004-2010 and girls' basketball in 2010-2011.
Dr. Yates, a 2002 graduate of Bluffton High School, earned his Bachelor's Degree in Business Education from Ball State University in 2006. He earned his Master of Science in Administrative Leadership from Indiana-Purdue University at Fort Wayne in 2009.  In 2018, Dr. Yates earned his Doctorate of Philosophy in Educational Leadership from Indiana State University.
Throughout his career in school administration, Dr. Yates has served as the Chairman, Vice Chairman, and Finance Chair of the School Employees’ Benefit Trust from 2014 through present, and he continues to serve as a Trustee and legislative liaison for the Trust.  As an active member of professional organizations in the state, Dr. Yates maintains membership with the Indiana Association of Public School Superintendents, Indiana School Boards Association, Upper Wabash Valley Superintendent Study Council, and the Indiana Association of School Business Officials (IASBO).  Beginning with the 2022-2023 school year, he has served as the Chairman for the Upper Wabash Valley Superintendent Study Council.
As part of IASBO, Dr. Yates completed the Chief Business Officer certification program in August of 2013.  In 2018, he was named the Region 3 IASBO School Business Official of the Year.  In addition, he served on the Board of Directors for IASBO as the Region 3 Director from 2018 to 2022.  Currently, Dr. Yates serves on the IASBO Human Resources & Management Research Committee and the Legislative Committee for the organization.
Locally, Dr. Yates has served on the Board of Directors for Bluffton Regional Medical Center since 2019.  In addition for over a decade (2012), Dr. Yates has served on the Board of Directors for the Bluffton-Southern Wells Dollars for Scholars.  As a representative of BHMSD, he has served on the Wells County Chamber of Commerce Board of Directors.  Beyond the duties as superintendent, Dr. Yates assists future educational leaders through his efforts as an adjunct faculty member teaching school finance at Purdue University Fort Wayne.
Dr. Yates and his wife, Jessica, live in Bluffton with their four children, Bailey, Addison, Deacon, and Liam.
Mrs. Julie Meitzler became Assistant Superintendent of Bluffton-Harrison Metropolitan School District in July of 2019. Previously, Mrs. Meitzler taught grades two-four, was an Assistant Principal at Bluffton-Harrison Elementary School, and Principal at Bluffton-Harrison Elementary School for nine years. Mrs. Meitzler has served BHMSD in various capacities for 39 years.
Mrs. Meitzler earned a Bachelor's Degree in Elementary Education from Ball State University in 1986 and has since earned her Master's Degree and obtained her Administrator's License from IPFW. In 2003, Mrs. Meitzler received the IPFW School Administration Award.
Mrs. Meitzler resides in Bluffton as an active member of the community. She has served on the United Way of Wells County Board and the Wells County Public Library Board. Mrs. Meitzler currently serves on the Park Center Board of Directors and the Imagine Early Board for Wells County. Additionally, Mrs. Meitzler is a member of the Indiana Association of School Principals and has served as an officer for District 3 where she was named district principal of the year in 2017.
Julie resides in Bluffton with her husband, Mike. She is a mother of two grown children and a grandmother to two grandchildren.  Mrs. Meitzler enjoys golfing and reading great books!

Ms. Abi West serves as the executive director of special education services at Adams Wells Special Services Cooperative (AWSSC). Ms. West joined the administrative team at AWSSC in July of 2018, having previously served one year as a special education coordinator in Knox County Special Education Cooperative (KCSEC). While at KCSEC, Ms. West served as an IRCA Autism Leader and Transition Coordinator. Additionally, Ms. West worked as a mild intervention special education teacher for a combined ten years between Eastbrook Community Schools and Marion Community Schools.
Ms. West is a voting member of the Council for Exceptional Children (CEC), Council of Administrators of Special Education (CASE), and Indiana Council of Administrators of Special Education (ICASE). Ms. West also serves on the ICASE Executive Committee as NorthEast RoundTable Representative since March 2024. In addition, Ms. West served as IIEP Stakeholder Representative from January 2019 - June 2021 and ICASE IIEP Stakeholder Representative from July 2021 – December 2023. At that time, Ms. West transitioned to the role of consultant with IDOE and PowerSchool in the development of Special Programs.
Ms. West earned both her master’s degree in special education mild interventions (2010) and Director of Exceptional Learners (2016) from Ball State University. Her undergraduate degree in secondary education – social studies was completed at Indiana University – Purdue University (IPFW).
Ms. Abi West is a Bluffton native with two adult daughters, Shelby and Megan Boyle. Ms. West enjoys gardening and spoiling her finicky felines and Cavalier King Charles Spaniels.

Phil Kurut has been the School Resource Officer (SRO) at BHMSD since 2018, and he has been with the Bluffton Police Department (BPD) since 2013.
Officer Kurut teaches BHMSD students DARE and pedestrian, traffic, and bike safety. He has also provided various safety training opportunities for BHMSD staff. Officer Kurut instructs defensive tactics, active shooter response, and de-escalation tactics for BPD, and he also serves as the Wellness Officer for the department.
Officer Kurut is a proud graduate of Bluffton High School, and he received a bachelor’s
degree from Ball State University. In his time serving as BHMSD’s SRO, Officer Kurut has been able to use his knowledge and expertise to help maintain the safety of the students, staff, and community.
