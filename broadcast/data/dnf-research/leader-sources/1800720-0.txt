We welcome all students to experience the BHMSD difference. We offer a variety of services, programs and extracurriculars to meet your child's needs and interests at each building.
From Fred F. Park Field on a fall Indiana night to the Tiger Den for a home basketball game, come cheer on the Tigers as our student athletes represent BHMSD.
BHMSD offers morning and afternoon pre-school programs that have a Paths to Quality Level 3 rating. The faculty and staff of BHMSD Tiger Cubs’ Preschool are committed to encouraging learning as a lifelong endeavor. Childcare is also available onsite at Bluffton-Harrison Elementary.
BHMSD serves breakfast and lunch daily. Printable lunch menus are updated monthly. Funds for accounts can be added online.
The BHMSD Board of School Trustees meets monthly to discuss and decide matters related to the district's operations. Meetings are livestreamed and minutes are made available to the public.
BHMSD maintains bus routes for students within the district. Learn more about bus assignments, rider policies and how to be come a BHMSD bus driver.
