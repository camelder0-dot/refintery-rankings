Superintendent's Message
As we begin another school year, I want to welcome our students, families, staff, and community back to Tippecanoe Valley School Corporation. A new year brings new opportunities to learn, grow, and succeed together.
At TVSC, our mission is Student Success through Literacy, Leadership, and Character Development. We remain committed to providing every student with a safe, supportive, and engaging learning environment where they are challenged to reach their full potential.
Success is built through strong partnerships between our schools, families, and community. Together, we will continue to do whatever it takes to equip ALL students to be successful today, tomorrow, and beyond.
Thank you for your continued support of Tippecanoe Valley Schools. I look forward to an outstanding year filled with learning, growth, and student success!!
Sincerely,
