Welcome to North Miami Community Schools!
It is a true honor to serve as your Superintendent. From the moment I visited our schools, I could feel the pride, tradition, and strong sense of community that make North Miami such a special place.
I believe every student deserves to be known, challenged, and supported to reach their fullest potential. Together, we will build on the great work already taking place while creating new opportunities for every Warrior to learn, grow, and succeed.
I look forward to meeting our families, students, staff, and community members, celebrating your successes, and working together to ensure North Miami remains a place where every child can thrive.
Thank you for welcoming me into the North Miami family. I am excited for all that we will accomplish together and look forward to serving this wonderful community.
With gratitude,
Dr. Jill VanDriessche
Superintendent
