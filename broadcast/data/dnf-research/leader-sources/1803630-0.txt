At Fort Wayne Community Schools, we are committed to transforming education to ensure students are ready for life after graduation, whether they enroll in higher education, enlist in the military or enter the workforce as an employee or entrepreneur. Through our
Schools of Success
, students connect their core academic studies with real world relevancy. In high school, this is accentuated by our partnership with
Junior Achievement's 3DE
, which students experience as soon as they enter the
Freshman Schools of Success
. From the moment students enter FWCS, they will see themselves in the Portrait of an Explorer (elementary), Portrait of a Connector (middle school) and
Portrait of a Graduate
(high school).
Students in FWCS are assigned to a school based on their residence. Families may also choose a school outside their attendance area by filling out a school choice lottery application.

FWCS opens doors to community leaders for Principal for a Day
Fort Wayne Community Schools welcomes more than 50 community members into its schools to serve as guest principals for the annual Principal for a Day event.
Fort Wayne Community Schools welcomes more than 50 community members into its schools to serve as guest principals for the annual Principal for a Day event. Guest principals, including community partners, state lawmakers, business leaders and pastors will visit their assigned schools the morning of Thursday, Oct. 8.
The guest principals will start their day greeting students and then spend the rest of the morning experiencing what a principal goes through in a day. They will also learn about Fort Wayne Community Schools’ Schools of Success initiative, which prepares students in pre-K through 12th grade to thrive after graduation in college, the military, entrepreneurship or the workforce by connecting core academics to real-world, career-focused experiences. Guest principals will learn about the Guaranteed Experiences afforded to all students in elementary, middle and high school and witness how Junior Achievement’s 3DE model brings businesses into the classroom at the high school level.
At noon, the principals and their guests will gather at the Wendy Y. Robinson Family & Community Engagement Center for a lunch prepared and served by FWCS Career Academy Culinary Arts students.

MidWest America makes further investment in FWCS
MidWest America Federal Credit Union is further investing in Fort Wayne Community Schools students through a new partnership with FWCS and the FWCS Foundation.
MidWest America Federal Credit Union is further investing in Fort Wayne Community Schools students through a new partnership with FWCS and the FWCS Foundation.
The partnership includes more than $60,000 in new support for schools, teachers and student programs across the district. MidWest America is providing $10,000 each to FWCS’ STEAM schools: Memorial Park Middle School, Weisser Park Elementary and Whitney Young Early Childhood Center.
The credit union is also donating $20,000 to the FWCS Foundation to support teacher grants. The teacher grant funding will provide 40 $500 classroom grants for teachers at Adams, Brentwood, Harrison Hill and Weisser Park elementary schools and Whitney Young Early Childhood Center.
“Partnerships like this create opportunities for our students and provide our teachers with additional resources to bring learning to life,” Fort Wayne Community Schools Superintendent Dr. Mark Daniel said. “We appreciate MidWest America’s investment in our schools and, most importantly, its commitment to the students and families we serve.”
Check presentations will take place Wednesday, Oct. 7, beginning at 8 a.m. at Memorial Park, 2200 Maumee Ave., followed by Weisser Park, 902 Colerick St., at 9 a.m. and Whitney Young, 1026 E. Pontiac St., at 10 a.m.
MidWest America is also supporting the Memorial Park music program and Big Noise, helping provide jackets for FWCS Student Ambassadors and continuing its involvement with programs including Schools of Success powered by 3DE and Futures ON Track.
“The FWCS Foundation exists to connect the generosity of our community with opportunities that make a difference for students and teachers,” said Rena Shown, Executive Director of the FWCS Foundation. “MidWest America’s support is a wonderful example of what can happen when a community partner believes in our schools and is willing to invest directly in the people inside them.”
Through the partnership, MidWest America and the FWCS Foundation are working together to expand opportunities for students and provide additional resources to schools across the district.
About the FWCS Foundation:
The Fort Wayne Community Schools Foundation connects community support with FWCS students, teachers and classrooms. Through donations and partnerships, the Foundation helps provide programs, resources and opportunities that make a difference for students across the district.
The FWCS Foundation is a separate 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization that works alongside Fort Wayne Community Schools to support educational programs and new ideas that help students learn, grow and prepare for their futures.
For more information, contact Executive Director Rena Shown at rena.shown@fwcs.k12.in.us.
FWCS students join National Walk & Roll to School Day
Fort Wayne Community Schools will celebrate National Walk & Roll to School Day with four schools participating.
Fort Wayne Community Schools will celebrate National Walk & Roll to School Day Wednesday, Oct. 7, with four schools participating.
Students and staff will gather at designated locations and walk together to school. The event aims to highlight the importance of safe and active travel for students, while reminding motorists of their role in ensuring the safety of those heading to school on streets and sidewalks.
The Walk to School Day events are supported by the Fort Wayne Active Transportation Coalition, which encourages making transportation safe and accessible in all forms.
The FWCS event is one of more than 2.800 events taking place in 47 states across the country under the guidance of the National Center for Safe Routes to School.
FWCS schools participating are:
•Arlington Elementary School
•Indian Village Elementary School
•Memorial Park Middle School
•St. Joseph Central Elementary School
FWCS Holiday Tournament returns to the court
The FWCS Holiday Tournament will tip off once again this December, featuring boys and girls basketball teams from all five FWCS high schools.
A Fort Wayne Community Schools tradition is returning to the court.
The FWCS Holiday Tournament will tip off once again this December, featuring boys and girls basketball teams from all five FWCS high schools. The two-day tournament will be held Tuesday, Dec. 22, and Wednesday, Dec. 23.
The tournament was canceled in October 2020 because of COVID-19 concerns and has not been held since.
“We are excited to bring back the FWCS Holiday Tournament,” Superintendent Dr. Mark Daniel said. “It is an opportunity for our student-athletes to compete and for students, families, alumni and community members to come together and support our schools. We look forward to making this event part of the holiday season once again.”
The tournament will be played at two locations. South Side High School will host the first day of competition Dec. 22, with the tournament moving to North Side High School for the second day Dec. 23.
Additional information, including game times, matchups and ticket information, will be announced at a later date.
