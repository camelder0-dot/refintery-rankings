Superintendent
It is an exciting time to be a part of Celina City Schools! As I enter into another year serving as superintendent, I am continually reminded of the strong foundation that exists within our district.
Our district is experiencing an important period of transition and opportunity as our PK-12 students are brought together within two buildings: Celina Elementary and Celina High School. This structure affords us greater opportunities for collaboration, shared staffing, and the thoughtful consolidation of resources. Most importantly, it gives us opportunities to work differently and more intentionally in ways that can benefit our students and strengthen the educational experience we provide.
I am a firm believer in the power of collaboration and partnerships—both within the walls of our schools and throughout our community. Nothing great happens in isolation. By bringing people, talents, and resources together, we can provide the support and opportunities our students need to succeed and truly Build Excellence Together. Our mission and vision call on us to challenge, prepare, and empower our students. To accomplish this, we must also be willing to challenge ourselves, prepare for shifts in our instruction, educational practices, and operations. As our district evolves, so do we in order to serve our students today, tomorrow, and into the foreseeable future. I am excited about what lies ahead for Celina City Schools. We have a strong foundation, a dedicated staff, supportive families and community partners, and tremendous opportunities in front of us. There is no limit on what we can accomplish!
