What Makes Us Great
We are driven by our unwavering commitment to excellence, equity, and the holistic development of our students. Together, we forge a path of educational success, inspiring a love for learning and empowering our students to shape a bright and promising future for themselves and our community.
Close-knit community
A smaller district where every student is known, supported, and encouraged to succeed.
Future-focused learning
Innovative programs and technology that prepare students for college, careers, and life.
Well-rounded opportunities
Strong academics paired with athletics, arts, and extracurriculars that help students discover their strengths.
Proud Panther tradition
A community that rallies around its schools and celebrates student achievements.

About Us
Our Mission + Vision
Our Goals
At Eastbrook Schools, we are committed to inspiring excellence in academics, character, and community. Serving families across Grant County, we provide a supportive, student-centered environment where every learner is known, valued, and challenged to reach their full potential. Through strong partnerships, dedicated staff, and a focus on growth, Eastbrook prepares students for success in school and beyond.
MISSION:
Eastbrook Community Schools Corporation provides a safe and stimulating educational environment, which prepares students for continuous learning and productive citizenship in a dynamic world.
VISION:
The vision of the Eastbrook Community Schools is to equip students to achieve academic success and to become productive adults.
All students within the Eastbrook Community Schools will demonstrate continuous academic growth and achievement.
All students within the Eastbrook Community Schools will demonstrate a commitment to being an effective member of their community by taking full responsibility for their actions and through demonstrating proper respect and service to their community, and most importantly, the people within their community.
