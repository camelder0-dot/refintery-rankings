Meet Our Staff
Mindy Schulz
mindy@wclaohio.org
Superintendent
With more than 25 years of diverse educational experience Mindy serves as Superintendent of the West Central Learning Academy. Prior to this, Mindy served as an executive director, elementary principal, teacher, and graduate level adjunct instructor.  She has also been a state trainer for the Ohio Department of Education  for all of the educator evaluation systems. She has presented numerous times at statewide conferences, and worked with educators across the state to implement various legislative mandates.
Maria Rellinger
maria@wclaohio.org
Treasurer
Maria Rellinger is a seasoned Treasurer serving WCLA and the Apollo Career Center.  Prior she served Paulding Schools, Continental Schools, and also was a State Auditor.    Maria has led and served on many educational committees, executive boards, and non-profits around the state.  She received a Bachelor degree in Business Administration from Ohio Northern University, and an MBA from the University of Findlay.  She currently resides in Kalida, Ohio with her husband and two children.
John Edinger
john@wclaohio.org
Principal
John has 39 years of experience in education, with the last 18 in administration.   He has been a High School Principal at Delphos Jefferson and a Middle School Principal at Spencerville. Prior to stepping into the administration role, John was a 7-12 math teacher.  He received his Bachelor of Arts through Bluffton University and his Masters in Administration through Bowling Green State University.
John is a sports enthusiast as he has coached multiple sports and is currently coaching football. In his spare time, he and his wife Sharon enjoy hanging out with family.

Melissa Prichard
melissa@wclaohio.org
English/Language Arts Teacher
Hi, I’m Mrs. P! I’m so excited to be a part of the WCLA family this year! I’m entering my 38th year in education, and I can honestly say that I still have the same passion and excitement for teaching as I did when I first started. Over the years, I’ve had the joy of teaching English for 18 years and serving as a counselor for 19 years at Spencerville. With 37 years of experience, you can trust that your child is in good hands!
My classroom is a place where curiosity thrives, and every student is encouraged to be their authentic self. I strive to create a welcoming, stress-free environment where students feel comfortable exploring new ideas and developing the skills they need for a bright future. It’s my mission to help them discover the best version of themselves along the way.
When I’m not in the classroom, you can find me enjoying time with my family, going for walks with my dog, listening to podcasts, or making memories with my grandkids.
This year is going to be an exciting journey, and I can’t wait to see what we accomplish together!
