Office of the Superintendent
The Office of the Superintendent provides strategic leadership and vision for Huntington County Community School Corporation, guiding the district toward excellence in education for all students. The Superintendent works closely with the school board, administrative team, staff, families, and community partners to establish goals, develop policies, allocate resources, and make decisions that shape the future of our schools. This office serves as the central hub for district-wide initiatives, community engagement, legislative advocacy, and organizational leadership. Through transparent communication and collaborative decision-making, the Superintendent's office ensures that all stakeholders work together toward the shared mission of preparing every student for success in college, career, and life.
