St. Joseph County ISD
The St. Joseph County ISD Provides services to our local district partners to provide exceptional educational services to the students of St. Joseph County.
Mission
Building Better Futures for Children and Communities.
Vision
Ensuring student success with community partnerships, an emphasis on integrity, and a growth mindset.

A Message from the Superintendent
Welcome to St. Joseph County ISD!
The purpose of an intermediate school district (ISD) is to provide support to the development and success of all students, either through direct programming or indirect support to local districts. From this perspective of servant leadership, we are here to serve our community.

Testimonials
Ben Karle:  Behavioral Health Program Coordinator
“I love trying to have an impact on my community. I'm fortunate to work with many wonderful people here at the ISD! Being able to help students see the possibilities in St. Joseph County and beyond is a big factor in my 10 years spent working at the St. Joseph County ISD!”
Trudy Camp: Adaptive Physical Education Teacher
“SJCISD has fostered my development as an educator from the very beginning, fresh from college 35 years ago. I have been provided ample opportunities to attend conferences and trainings every year to stay current and connected in my field. Guidance from other teachers and administrators as well as experiences and student inspiration along the way fortified my passion to lead learning.”
Cara Kiley: Speech-Language Pathologist
“I have worked at the ISD for 16 years. I continue to work at the ISD because I love working with kids and have made strong relationships with the families and coworkers in my district and at the ISD. The ISD allows the local districts in our county to have access to a multitude of special education and general education supports and services.”
Nicole Fetterer: Physical Therapist
"I have been working as a Physical Therapist at the ISD for the past six years. What keeps me motivated to continue my journey here is the incredible opportunity to make a positive impact on the lives and education of our students. Every day, I come to work with a sense of purpose, knowing that I can contribute to their growth and overall well-being."
