Administration & Business
Welcome to the Administration & Business division of Huntington County Community Schools. Our dedicated team works behind the scenes to ensure the smooth operation of our district, supporting students, staff, and families every day. From managing human resources and financial operations to overseeing communications and insurance services, this division provides the essential infrastructure that allows our schools to focus on what matters most—student success. Whether you're seeking employment opportunities, need to connect with district leadership, have questions about business services, or want to stay informed about district news and initiatives, you'll find the resources and support you need here. We're committed to transparency, fiscal responsibility, and excellent service to our school community.
