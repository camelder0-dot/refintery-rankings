Business Services
Our Business Services Department is responsible for the management and supervision of our district’s business affairs as they relate to budget allocation, fiscal reporting, finances, accounting, payroll, purchasing, accounts payable, and other such services. The department members perform these duties with integrity and with a focus on fiscal responsibility. We welcome you to contact the Superintendent with any questions you may have about our district budget or about the allocation of funds.
If you are a prospective or current vendor, please feel free to contact us by phone or email, and we will assist you in any way we can.

Teacher Compensation Package
At Madison-Grant United School Corporation, we understand that the classroom teacher has the greatest impact on student learning. That is why we must remain competitive in teacher compensation while staying fiscally responsible. Often, people focus only on salary, or the amount one is paid. Many additional expenses make up our teacher compensation package. Our school district contributes the following benefits to each of our teachers:
80% of Health Insurance premiums
50% of Dental Insurance premiums
Cover the 3% teacher contribution to TRF
Provide Long-term Disability Insurance for $1 to the teacher
Provide a $50,000 Life Insurance policy for $1 to the teacher
Together, these salary and benefits contributions cost more than $10 million annually. Our people are our greatest resource in positively impacting student lives from newborn through graduation.
