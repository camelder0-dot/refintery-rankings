Mr. Aaron McClure named as next Superintendent
Adams Central Community Schools
October 15, 2025
The school board is pleased to announce that Mr. Aaron McClure has been approved to be the next superintendent of schools for Adams Central Community Schools. Mr. McClure has served Adams Central in various roles for the past 27 years. He will begin transitioning into the role of superintendent and officially assume it on January 1, 2026. For more information about Mr. McClure and the superintendent search process and transition, please visit our website at https://www.accs.k12.in.us/page/supertransition
