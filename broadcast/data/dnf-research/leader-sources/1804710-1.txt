Administration
Leading Excellence in Educational Leadership
Administrative roles at Huntington County Community Schools are designed for visionary leaders who are passionate about educational excellence and student success. Our administrators work collaboratively to create positive school cultures, implement innovative programs, and support both staff and students in achieving their highest potential. From building-level leadership to district-wide coordination, our administrative positions offer opportunities to shape educational policy, mentor staff, and directly impact the learning environment. We seek leaders who demonstrate strong communication skills, data-driven decision-making, and a commitment to continuous improvement. Our administrative team works closely with teachers, families, and community members to ensure that every student receives a quality education in a safe and supportive environment.
