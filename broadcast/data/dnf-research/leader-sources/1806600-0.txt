Locations
A Thriving Community
to Call Home
Located in the heart of Elkhart County, Middlebury Community Schools spans 100 square miles of vibrant neighborhoods and a robust manufacturing economy. Our district offers the perfect blend of rural charm and modern convenience, with easy access to major urban centers for culture and higher education. Whether you are new to the area or a lifelong resident, our seven-school campus system is designed to be the heartbeat of this growing community.
