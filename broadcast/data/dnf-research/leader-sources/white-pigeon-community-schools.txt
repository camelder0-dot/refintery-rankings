Superintendent
Shelly McBride
Welcome to White Pigeon Community Schools,
As the superintendent of White Pigeon Community Schools, I am dedicated to fostering a learning environment that promotes both academic excellence and community pride. Rooted in our tradition of "Chief Pride," I take great honor in respecting the rich history of White Pigeon while leading our district forward with innovation and purpose.
Our strategic plan emphasizes building strong relationships with students, staff, and families, ensuring we meet the needs of our community in a safe and caring environment. With a focus on rigorous academic standards, equitable opportunities, and engaging learning experiences, we strive to prepare every student for success in their chosen path while equipping them with the skills they need for life beyond the classroom. Whether they choose to enter college, a vocational training program, the workforce, or pursue other opportunities, our graduates will be ready.
While we honor the values and traditions that define White Pigeon, I am committed to driving progress. Through transparent communication, a focus on social-emotional learning, and initiatives like recruitment and retention of talented staff, we are creating a future where "Chief Pride" continues to thrive and evolve.
Thank you for your continued support and commitment to our students and community. I look forward to the great things we will accomplish together.
Sincerely,
Shelly McBride
Superintendent, White Pigeon Community Schools
