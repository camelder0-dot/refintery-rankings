About our District
Fort Recovery Local Schools:
Better today than you were yesterday. Better tomorrow than you were today!
About Our District
Fort Recovery Local is a small school district that takes pride in the past, present, and future. Our students and staff work hard to be one of the top schools in the state of Ohio. Educating around one-thousand students, our small, family-oriented district relies on a strong community to perform at high levels.
Mission
The mission of the Fort Recovery Local School District is to create a culture in which students, staff, and community work, learn, and achieve together to maximize student opportunities.
Beliefs
We believe the school shares the value of and is the heart of the community.
We believe that timely and regular communication is vital to our success as a district.
We believe in providing diverse and multiple opportunities in the area of co-curricular and extra-curricular to all students.
We believe the staff and students need to possess the agility to be prepared for the unknown.
