Butler Elementary
"Be Respectful, Responsible and Ready"
Butler Elementary is proud to offer students and staff a safe and caring learning environment. We believe that everyone in our building should be respectful, responsible, and ready to learn every day. We expect all learners to grow academically and be the best they can be. We welcome all learners and strive to create a positive learning environment for all!

Riverdale Elementary
“The choice is YOURS!”
Riverdale Elementary is a small, family-oriented school in rural St. Joe, Indiana. We believe all of our children can begin the pursuit of life-long learning in our student-centered environment. “Have a GREAT DAY, or not. The choice is YOURS!!!”
