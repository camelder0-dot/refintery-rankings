Why Us
Small School, Big Opportunities
From athletics to co-curricular activities and community partnerships, we are proud of the collaborations we have developed to offer a wide variety of opportunities to our students within our rural school setting.
Faculty Advanced Degrees
52% of Adams Central's faculty have an advanced education degree.
Average Class Size
Our elementary students thrive in class sizes that average 21 students.  Our middle school averages 20 students per class and with students selecting various learning paths, our high school class sizes average 16 students.
Academic Success
Academic Success is a point of pride.  From our high middle school math scores to the number of students who achieve perfect iRead scores, and the high rate at which our students earn dual-enrollment credits.
