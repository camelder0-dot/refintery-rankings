New Leadership at Heritage
Posted Date: 10/14/26 (07:03 AM)
View Original Post
We are excited to announce the new leadership team at Heritage Intermediate School!
Kelly Anglemyer has been named principal after serving the past eight years as Heritage’s assistant principal. His knowledge of Heritage and strong commitment to its students, staff, and families will serve him well as he steps into this new role.
Joining him as assistant principal is Jessica Barkby. She is currently serving as an assistant principal with Wawasee Community Schools, but is no stranger to Middlebury. Prior to moving into administration, Jessica spent more than 20 years teaching in Middlebury Community Schools with the majority of those spent at Heritage.
We are confident Mr. Anglemyer and Mrs. Barkby will be a great leadership team for Heritage and are excited to see them work together to serve the school community.
Please join us in congratulating Mr. Anglemyer and welcoming Mrs. Barkby back to Middlebury Community Schools. We are excited about what is ahead!
