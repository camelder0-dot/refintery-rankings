Welcome!
As Superintendent of Argos Community Schools, it is my great pleasure to welcome you to our school community—where tradition, excellence, and heart come together to create something truly special.
Argos may be small in size, but we are mighty in spirit. Our dedicated staff, talented students, and supportive families form a close-knit school community that values every child and every voice. We pride ourselves on providing a high-quality education tailored to the individual needs of our students, while encouraging them to grow, achieve, and succeed in a rapidly changing world.
Our teachers are hardworking and deeply committed. They go above and beyond to build strong relationships, inspire learning, and invest in the whole child. Whether in academics, the arts, athletics, or career pathways, we strive to offer opportunities for every student to explore their strengths, discover their passions, and build a strong foundation for the future.
At Argos, we are more than just a school—we are a family. Our community plays a vital role in who we are, and we are grateful for the continued support, encouragement, and pride shown by our families, local businesses, and community partners. Together, we are building something truly meaningful for our students and the future of Argos.
Thank you for visiting our website. I invite you to explore all that Argos Community Schools has to offer and see for yourself how Dragon pride is alive in every classroom, every program, and every student we serve.
With Pride and Purpose,
Kerry Johnson
Superintendent of Schools
