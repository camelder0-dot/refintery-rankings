# Third-party sources

Team identities, competition statistics, public school affiliations, and publicly documented leadership roles are attributed in the data and source catalog.

The preserved Statbotics source files and excerpts are from the pinned upstream commit identified in their manifest. Their upstream MIT license is retained beside them. The compressed Statbotics response is a factual data snapshot, with its original URL, response headers, retrieval time, and checksum.

The FTC companion directory preserves factual FIRST, FTCScout, NCES, and Census data extracts and response metadata. FTCScout's algorithm is attributed through pinned upstream source links; its code is not copied into this repository. Team portfolios and news articles remain linked to their publishers, with factual role extracts and access qualifications retained locally.

School presentations, meeting minutes, news reports, social-media posts, professional profiles, and podcasts are linked to their publishers. This repository records the relevant facts and qualifications rather than republishing those sources in full. The document manifest identifies the linked PDF versions that were checked.

FIRST, Statbotics, FTCScout, school, team, and organization names belong to their respective organizations. Source links do not imply that any organization reviewed or endorsed this compilation. No new license for third-party material is asserted here.
